Shared build notes

Power & digital

The Control Unit is placed in every layout (green piece, start/finish). The Wireless+ charging straight appears where there's a low-speed zone. Lane-change pieces are positioned to give at least two passing opportunities per lap.

What stays in the box

Pit-lane turnouts, the pit adapter and the two single-lane straights aren't solver-verified yet (catalog solverReady:false) — add the pit lane as an off-circuit spur next to any start/finish straight if you want pit stops. Border end-pieces: put them on hairpin and banked-curve exits.
